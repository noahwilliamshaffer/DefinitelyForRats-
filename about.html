Who we are

Verity Research Labs supplies research peptides and supporting laboratory materials to researchers, laboratories, and academic and institutional buyers. We keep the catalogue deliberately small, so that every item on it can be specified, documented, and supported properly.

Our mission

To make research material straightforward to source: clearly specified, consistently packaged, and published with the documentation a laboratory needs to verify what it has received.

Quality and documentation

Every product is listed with its technical specifications and a certificate of analysis for the batch currently in stock. Certificates report identity and purity and the methods used to determine them, and are published on our Certificates of Analysis page, matched to each product by name.

Lyophilized peptides are supplied in sealed glass vials under vacuum, the standard form for stable storage and transport.

Research use only

All products are sold strictly for laboratory, academic, and institutional research. They are not intended for human dosing, injection, or ingestion, and are not for human or animal consumption. We do not provide dosing, usage, or protocol guidance of any kind, and we do not make medical or health claims about any product.

Purchasers must be 21 or older and agree to our Terms & Conditions, which confirm research-only use.
