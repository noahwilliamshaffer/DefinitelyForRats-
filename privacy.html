Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information [Your Brand Research LLC] collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

When you place an order we receive your name, email address, billing and shipping addresses, and the details of your order. Card details are entered on our payment processor's secure hosted page and are never received or stored by us.

Your cart and age confirmation are stored in your own browser (local and session storage) so the site works as you move between pages. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To process, ship, and support your order; to send order confirmations and shipping updates; to prevent fraud; and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to complete your order — our payment processor, shipping carriers, and website host — and authorities where the law requires it.

Retention

Order records are kept for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, then deleted.
